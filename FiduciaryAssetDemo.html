02 Critical Insurance Rules

Do NOT use the existing Homeowner's policy — you are not the insured as the deed has changed.

Do NOT have 2 policies existing simultaneously — your claim can be denied.

Have your policy started the day of closing.

Do NOT cancel existing policy until you close — closings get delayed.

The Insured is the Trustee: Fiduciary Asset Protection Group, LLC (not the Trust — the Trust is a document).

Name the Bank as the 1st mortgagee.

Sellers as Additional Interest (NOT Additionally Insured) — seller's name will not be on insurance claim check.

Cover Beneficiary with General Liability Insurance — declaration page is NOT sent to the bank.

Cover the Seller with General Liability Insurance — and send that to the bank.

If existing policy is escrowed (PiTi), have the insurance company send the invoice to the bank to be paid.

You must have a Durable Power of Attorney (POA) to sign claim checks with the seller's name.

⚠️

YOU SHOULD NEVER CLOSE WITHOUT INSURANCE

The existing seller's insurance does NOT cover you.
